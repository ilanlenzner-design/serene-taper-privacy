Frequently Asked Questions

How do I switch between Provider and Patient mode?

On the home screen, tap Provider Mode or Patient Mode. You can also switch modes from the settings screen inside either mode.

How does a provider share a plan with a patient?

In Provider Mode, create a taper plan and tap Share Plan. The patient receives a code they can enter in Patient Mode under Import Plan.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your taper data is stored securely and only shared between a provider and their specific patient when using the plan-sharing feature. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

The app isn't loading my plan — what should I do?

Make sure you have an internet connection and that the plan code was entered correctly. If the problem persists, email us with your device type and iOS version.
