Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Downshift Health ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application ("App") handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

Downshift Health is designed with privacy in mind. The App operates primarily on your device and does not require account registration. We may collect the following:

  • Tapering plan data: Medication names, dosage schedules, and tapering progress that you or your healthcare provider enter into the App. This data is stored locally on your device and optionally synced via Supabase (our secure backend) only when you use the plan-sharing feature.
  • Usage data: Basic, anonymized analytics to help us improve the App (e.g., which screens are visited). No personally identifiable information is included.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display your tapering schedule and track your progress
  • To allow healthcare providers to share plans with patients
  • To improve the App's functionality and user experience

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your medication and tapering data is only shared between a healthcare provider and their patient when both parties use the plan-sharing feature within the App.

4. Data Storage and Security

Data stored via our backend (Supabase) is encrypted in transit and at rest. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. You may delete your data at any time by removing the App or contacting us.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase – backend data storage
  • Expo / React Native – app framework

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
